## Summary (Granola)

**Music Nerd Labs and team updates**

- The Labs site is live at lab.musicnerd.net, an outcome of Pete's recent onsite. It showcases experiments built on Music Nerd's foundation. Each experiment's prompts are open and copyable ("how is this made"). The Web3 music NFT documentary showcase was a standout from the onsite.
- Suggestion: add GitHub links for open-source codebases alongside the prompts, to make experiments fully reproducible.
- Pete is shifting toward more outreach work (socials, Substack) to close out the year.
- Carl built an iOS background photo uploader for InProcess using OpenAI's Dot agent, with no manual coding. Like Google Photos, photos added to an InProcess album in the Photos app upload automatically. UI polish is underway, with TestFlight targeted for the weekend.

**Codebase and infrastructure priorities**

- The API/Web split is nearly complete: of about 16–20 server actions, the last few finish by Friday end of day.
- The Vercel Board is up but open to the world, so it needs auth. Plan: Privy auth with an email allowlist (CY, Carl, Pete, Sweetman, Jade). Agents also need to authenticate and get agent codes to post. The goal is for agents to communicate through the board, with humans using it too (for example a code-review channel replacing Discord). Evaluate over the next week whether agents actually use it or it gets shelved.
- Tech debt to consider for the rest of the year: Supabase per-branch preview databases (instead of every preview using the staging DB); a CI review confirming baseline functionality and regression coverage on every merge to main; and agent auto-merge, with enough test and eval infrastructure that agents can merge their own PRs. Auto-merge is the biggest stretch goal; the benchmark is teams shipping 4,000+ PRs a month.

**InProcess: chain strategy and payment options**

- Plan: move from Base to Ethereum mainnet once the hard fork ships (delayed from November to January). The threshold is under $0.01 gas per contract deployment; $0.03 is borderline. Base paymasters currently cost under a cent to publish a collection.
- The case for Ethereum is 11 years without downtime, versus Base outages, and Ethereum building for permanence. The counter-view is that agents will abstract chains away by picking the most cost-effective one. Sweetman owns InProcess chain decisions.
- Peer.xyz (formerly ZK P2P) is flagged as possible payment infrastructure. Zero-knowledge proofs verify fiat transfers (Wise, Zelle, Venmo, Cash App and others), then release USDC. Artists could receive payment however they like while InProcess settles on-chain. It goes in the backlog for now.
- InProcess likely folds into Music Nerd, with at least deep app-layer links between the two. The branding decision is deferred; it is de facto part of Music Nerd, given the shared team and codebase.
