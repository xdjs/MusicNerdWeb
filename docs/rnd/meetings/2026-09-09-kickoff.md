## Product

Pete is running the project day to day with Carl and CY advising. The mission is to turn
passive listening into engaged curiosity. Music Nerd Web is the artist profile and research
database and the current focus; it is a researcher and, in part, a publicist, and should be the
source of truth for an artist's story and process. Music Nerd TV is the listener experience
built on top and is paused until the database layer is solid. Both should eventually consume a
shared Music Nerd API, which does not exist yet.

## Onboarding and research

An artist claims a profile, receives a reference code, and sends it to the Music Nerd
Instagram account for manual approval. On approval a research agent resolves social profiles
from the Deezer ID, reads interviews and articles, and pulls Instagram posts through Apify.
Music Nerd Web uses Tavily for search and Gemini for synthesis; Music Nerd TV uses Exa. A
wizard then walks the artist through About, Ask About, Links, Support links, and Lore. Every
lore claim links to its source. Artists can add URLs, upload files (JSON, Markdown, text, PDF,
10 MB limit), or trigger a web-source search. A three-question interview follows onboarding,
built from what the research found.

Gaps Pete called out: link categorization sends Apple Music and label links into lore rather
than Links; some found links are irrelevant; link reordering is not built; the "this year's
releases" section may be redundant; reindexing is manual with no cron; Exa and Tavily have not
been compared on cost or quality; the research loading UI is a static box, and Pete prefers
components streaming in as data arrives.
