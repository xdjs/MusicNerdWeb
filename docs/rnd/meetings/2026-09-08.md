## Artist feedback and immediate fixes

A large PDF upload failed against the reported 10 MB limit without adequate guidance. Pete
planned clearer size-limit messaging and better support for In Process links. The feedback
also reinforced the value of Latest as a way to surface material already in the profile.

### Decisions

**Accept feedback without promising every request.** Record it, select fixes deliberately and
keep product scope under the team's control. One demanding case can reveal real failures
without becoming the specification for every artist.

**Do not enable continuous backups at this point.** The team discussed the available backup
window after a missing biography. This was a dated operating decision, not a statement of the
current provider plan or retention configuration.

## Biography preservation and recovery

Carl suggested protecting human-edited bios from generation overwrites and asked Pete to test
a restore into a separate database instance to look for the missing content. A lock was a
proposal; the meeting did not establish that protection or recovery was already implemented.
No production restore is authorized by this historical note.
