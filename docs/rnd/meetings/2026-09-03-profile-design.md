## Profile layout and payoff

Tom and Pete placed Latest immediately after About as the next design direction, using recent
posts, releases and artist answers to make the page useful at a glance. MNTv cards were a visual
reference. Shorter biography text, section containers, navigation and header arrangement were
also explored. No complete final profile layout was signed off.

## Issues from the walkthrough

The guided flow worked with email login and a manually approved claim. Interview prompts
referenced prior posts and answers entered the knowledge document, but the artist needed a
more immediate payoff. Wizard placement failed on a smaller laptop viewport. Other feedback
included button-text contrast, the outdated Deezer logo, link grouping/reordering and making
form submission easier from the keyboard.

The date's R&D meeting revisited this recorded walkthrough. Its decision to call the section
Lore followed this design session's discussion of alternatives such as Vault and Canon.

## Integrations and account concepts

Sleeve integration raised song- and album-specific questions that the artist research path
might not answer. A separate song-research approach was left for exploration, not approved as
an immediate implementation. Artist profiles and fan accounts retained distinct purposes.

## Community design

Tom approved the tape-and-collage direction for Feed Forward materials. Event timing was still
being coordinated; use later meeting notes and the calendar rather than the source's ambiguous
time-zone conversions.
