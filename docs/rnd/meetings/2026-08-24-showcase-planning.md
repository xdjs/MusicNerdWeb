## Showcase format

Pete and Black Dave chose an every-other-week showcase with room for feedback, co-working and
open discussion. Riverside was the preferred recording platform; its practical limits still
needed checking. Music and Tech Showcase was the working name, with an early-September start.
This updated the August 20 idea of separate monthly formats. Later sessions evolved into
Feed Forward; dates and names here belong to the planning stage.

## Profile walkthrough and feedback

The session included a guided staging walkthrough. It showed the value of automatically
assembled links and sources, while raising how to make participation worthwhile after the
initial profile is built. A guided walkthrough is not a cold usability test.

Audio answers, existing-interview imports, ongoing prompts and local-scene applications were
explored as ways to capture richer artist context. These are product ideas from this discussion,
not shipped features or a replacement architecture. The subsequent August 27 team meeting
sets the immediate underground-artist and profile-claiming priorities.
