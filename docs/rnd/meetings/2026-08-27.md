## The onboarding demo

Pete showed a flow that researches the artist before a guided profile review. The vault held
articles, links and material extracted from Instagram, but also included unwanted aggregators.
The Ask needed follow-up testing for recent activity, source pills and collaborator links.

### Decisions

**Focus on underground artists.** Carl and CY agreed that big-name artists are a non-goal for
this stage. Establish a useful experience for the artists the team can work with directly.

**Use chat for interview questions.** The initial mandatory chat can be removed while questions
remain available in the profile experience. An email interview cadence is not the first delivery
mechanism.

**Defer contribution mechanics beyond links.** Artist-curated profiles are the immediate focus;
a fan reputation or annotation system is not part of this phase.

**Make source quality explicit.** The source judge should consult a domain credibility list.
Boomplay and Clubhouse illustrated why an aggregator can be a poor source even when its name
matches. The subsequent implementation plan records the more specific exclusion policy.

**Make artist information readable by bots.** The profile and knowledge document should expose
useful substance to crawlers. Discussing broader MCP access did not settle its implementation.

## Artist activity and MNTv

Carl described activity as a reason for artists to keep curating and for listeners to return.
Collaborator updates and recent posts were possible ingredients, not a finished feature design.
The publicist analogy was useful: collect trustworthy material and give the artist something
worth using. Finish profile claiming before connecting Music Nerd TV to that work.
