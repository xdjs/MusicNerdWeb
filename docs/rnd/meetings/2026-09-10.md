## In Process integration

Carl proposed that artists who have made music NFTs can pull them into their profile, and
that the feature quietly pins the NFT so it stays available. Patrick asked whether to build
indexing or use In Process's existing index and API. Carl: use what exists; the team is
against not-invented-here. Patrick noted it would also expose gaps in the In Process index.

Pete added a longer-term idea: lore uploads should be posted to In Process or minted so they
survive if Music Nerd goes away, citing the loss of a Caribbean music blog and the press
history it held. Pete said this is not important now. Carl framed the whole area as two
directions: push from Music Nerd to In Process, and pull from In Process into Music Nerd.

### Decisions

**Patrick's first feature is displaying an artist's music NFTs on the profile using In
Process's existing index and API.** No new indexing. Preservation of the displayed NFTs is
part of the intent. — Carl, agreed by Patrick; scope and timing to be set Monday with Pete
